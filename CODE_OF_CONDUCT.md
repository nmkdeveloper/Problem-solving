# Code of Conduct

## Our Standards

We are committed to providing a welcoming and respectful environment for everyone. Contributions should be constructive, inclusive, and focused on learning and solving problems.

Examples of acceptable behavior include:

- Using respectful and professional language.
- Giving and receiving constructive feedback.
- Respecting different experience levels and approaches.
- Taking responsibility for mistakes and correcting them.

Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, discrimination, personal attacks, deliberate disruption, and sharing private information without permission.

## Enforcement

Report unacceptable behavior to the repository maintainers. All reports will be reviewed and handled fairly and confidentially where possible. Maintainers may remove comments, reject contributions, or restrict participation when necessary to protect the community.
